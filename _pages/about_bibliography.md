I am a Master's student at ETH Zurich majoring in electrical engineering. I am a visiting researcher at [Anima's Lab](http://tensorlab.cms.caltech.edu/users/anima/) at Caltech. Previously, I worked in [Alsemy](https://www.alsemy.com/), [CSEM](https://www.csem.ch/en/), [Learning & Adaptive Systems Group](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/) under Prof. Andreas Krause at ETH Zurich and [SAFARI Group](https://safari.ethz.ch/) under Prof. Onur Mutlu at ETH Zurich.

My current research is centered on **neural PDE solvers** and **operator learning** , with applications in solving partial differential equations, modeling semiconductor devices, and weather forecasting. For example, I am exploring the use of stochastic methods combined with deep learning to **solve high-dimensional Poisson equations** and developing an unsupervised, function-level anomaly detection method using neural operators to **identify erroneous transistor device simulations**. While I am deeply interested in proposing new machine learning algorithms, I am also passionate about scientific applications, particularly in areas such as modeling and optimizing the reliability of transistor devices, simulating thermal behaviors of transistors, improving weather forecasting models through function-level self-supervised learning, and accelerating page table translation.

I have had the fortunate opportunity to work across a wide range of fields, including (in chronological order) neuroscience, robotics, chip design, computer architecture, semiconductor physics, and deep learning. This diverse experience has shaped my broad interest in multi-disciplinary areas such as computational electronics + deep learning, robot learning, Systems4AI, and AI4EDA.
