I am a PhD student in data science at New York University (NYU), advised by [Zongyi Li](https://zongyi-li.github.io/). I also work with [Zenithon AI](https://zenithon.ai), building foundation models for plasma physics. Before NYU, I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in electrical engineering at ETH Zurich and was a visiting student in [Anima Anandkumar's lab](http://tensorlab.cms.caltech.edu/users/anima/) at Caltech.

My research centers on **operator learning** and **generative models**, with a focus on mathematical principles and physical correctness. I develop methods that learn maps between function spaces and generative models over functions. Recent examples are **energy-based operator learning** and **weakly supervised training of neural operators** from stochastic estimators. I have applied these ideas to scientific and engineering problems, including solving high-dimensional Poisson equations, simulating fluid dynamics in complex geometries, planning under noise, unsupervised function-level anomaly detection for transistor device simulations, and augmenting semiconductor device simulations with generative operators.

I care as much about scientific applications as about new machine learning algorithms. My earlier work includes modeling transistor reliability and thermal behavior, improving weather forecasting with function-level self-supervised learning, and accelerating virtual address translation in processors.

Previously, I worked at [Alsemy](https://www.alsemy.com/), [CSEM](https://www.csem.ch/en/), the [Learning & Adaptive Systems Group](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/) under Prof. Andreas Krause and the [SAFARI Group](https://safari.ethz.ch/) under Prof. Onur Mutlu at ETH Zurich, and the [Korea Military Academy](http://www.kma.ac.kr/). My path has taken me through neuroscience, robotics, chip design, computer architecture, semiconductor physics, and deep learning, in that order. This range draws me to multidisciplinary areas such as AI4Science, scientific foundation models, computational electronics with deep learning, Systems4AI, and AI4EDA.
